🛡️ Security, Privacy & Ringing Architecture

Engineered with zero-trust privacy, encrypted TURN media relays, and 4-tier alert redundancy.

🔒

Encrypted TURN Relay (Zero IP Exposure)

Enforces strict WebRTC iceTransportPolicy: 'relay' via TLS/TCP proxies. Direct peer-to-peer candidate exchange is disabled so neither homeowner nor visitor IP addresses, ISPs, or physical locations are ever exposed.

⏱️

Strict 10-Day Automated Retention

All visitor voice notes, call recordings, photo drop-offs, and activity logs are automatically purged by our daily database cutoff worker after 10 days. Homeowners can also instantly wipe all logs with one tap.

🔑

Placard Rotation & Anti-Abuse Guard

QR placards encode zero personal contact details. Homeowners can instantly revoke or rotate their placard from Settings. Signaled calls use single-use cryptographic UUIDs protected by server-side rate limits.

⚡

4-Tier Alert Redundancy Pipeline

Tier 1: Native APNs VoIP / FCM push. Tier 2: Simultaneous multi-device household ringing. Tier 3: 15-second fail-safe Twilio SMS/voice call backup. Tier 4: Offline visitor self-service fallback.
