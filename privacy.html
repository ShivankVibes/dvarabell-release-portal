DvaraBell Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 3, 2026 | Last Comprehensive Revision: November 2026

Privacy Commitment: DvaraBell was engineered from the ground up to protect both homeowners and visitors. We employ encrypted TURN relay proxies to completely hide your IP address, enforce a strict rolling 10-day retention cutoff on visitor notes and recordings, store zero live call media on our servers, and ensure physical QR placards encode zero personal contact details.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how DvaraBell ("we", "us", "our", operated by BLSS) collects, processes, protects, and disposes of personal data and communications when you use the DvaraBell mobile applications (iOS, Android), our web-based homeowner portals, and the visitor calling interface.

3. WebRTC Architecture: TURN Relays & IP Anonymization

RFC 8827 / W3C WEBRTC COMPLIANT

Standard WebRTC connections can inadvertently expose public IP addresses to other participants during direct peer-to-peer host candidate negotiation. DvaraBell completely eliminates this privacy tradeoff:

  • Enforced TURN Relay Policy: All WebRTC peer connections in DvaraBell strictly enforce iceTransportPolicy: 'relay'. Direct peer-to-peer host candidate exchange is suppressed.
  • Complete IP Address Masking: Neither the homeowner nor the visitor can ever inspect, log, or uncover each other’s public IP address, Internet Service Provider, or geographic location. All traffic flows through isolated, high-bandwidth TURN relay proxies over TLS/TCP (ports 443 and 80).
  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: Audio and video packets are encrypted using DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security) and SRTP (Secure Real-time Transport Protocol). Our signaling servers only facilitate the initial SDP handshake and have no ability to tap or inspect your live conversation.
  • Homeowner Privacy by Default: When answering a call, the homeowner’s camera is always disabled by default. Video is only transmitted if the homeowner explicitly chooses to tap the camera toggle.

4. QR Code Security, Revocation & Anti-Abuse Rate Limiting

Because outdoor QR placards are publicly accessible, DvaraBell incorporates multi-layered security controls against scanning abuse and unwanted ringing:

  • Instant QR Revocation & Rotation: If a placard is copied or compromised, homeowners can immediately revoke, rotate, or switch their active household code within the app Settings. Revoked codes immediately return a 404 Invalid Code response and can no longer ring your devices.
  • Cryptographic Ephemeral Sessions: Scanning a QR placard generates an isolated, cryptographically random single-use session ID (UUIDv4) that expires in 10 minutes. Calls automatically auto-decline if unanswered after 30 seconds.
  • Server-Side Rate Limiting: Our backend signaling APIs enforce strict IP and device rate limiters to prevent malicious automated ringing or DDoS attempts.
  • Busy-Line Guard: If a call is already in progress, subsequent visitor scans receive an immediate "Line Busy" screen and are invited to leave a note or photo rather than interrupting your active call.
  • Do-Not-Disturb & Silent Dismiss: Homeowners can decline or dismiss rings instantly without acknowledging or answering the visitor.

5. Visitor Consent & Call Recording Transparency

DvaraBell complies with strict international and domestic wiretapping and privacy regulations regarding call recording and audio notes:

  • Visitor Voice Notes: Voice notes are never captured passively. Visitors must explicitly interact with the "Record Voice Note" button, which displays an active recording timer and requires a conscious tap to submit to the homeowner.
  • Two-Way Call Recordings: When either a homeowner or visitor initiates recording during a live call:
    1. A prominent, flashing red recording indicator (🔴 REC) is immediately displayed on both participants’ screens.
    2. Real-time banners announce to both parties that audio/video recording is underway.
    3. Visitors are granted an immediate local copy of the recording (downloadable directly as MP4/MP3) upon call completion.
  • Automated 10-Day Expiry: All saved recordings are subjected to our automated 10-day retention cutoff, ensuring door recordings are never retained indefinitely.

6. Ringing Reliability & Multi-Tiered Redundancy

Standard doorbell push notifications can occasionally be affected by device battery savers or poor mobile cellular reception. DvaraBell implements a 4-tier redundancy pipeline to maximize delivery while maintaining full transparency:

  • Tier 1 — High-Priority Native Push & WebSockets: Apple VoIP PushKit (iOS) and High-Priority FCM Push + 24/7 Foreground Socket Service (Android) trigger full-screen incoming call UI. Android users are advised to whitelist DvaraBell from aggressive OEM battery optimization.
  • Tier 2 — Household Multi-Ring: Doorbell calls simultaneously ring all registered devices belonging to household family members or co-residents.
  • Tier 3 — Fail-Safe Twilio Alerts: If no device answers a call within 15 seconds, our servers automatically dispatch a backup SMS or automated voice call alert to the homeowner's designated mobile phone.
  • Tier 4 — Offline Visitor Fallback: If a homeowner is unreachable or offline, the visitor interface seamlessly transitions to the after-call screen, enabling visitors to leave a photo drop-off, voice note, text message, or package delivery instruction.

7. Infrastructure Security & Storage Regions

All DvaraBell services, APIs, and databases are hosted within certified, enterprise-grade cloud facilities (MongoDB Atlas and Render cloud infrastructure):

  • Encryption at Rest: All stored database volumes utilize hardware-accelerated AES-256 disk encryption.
  • Encryption in Transit: All web, mobile, and WebSocket traffic is protected via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS certificates with HSTS headers.
  • Third-Party Processors: We partner solely with industry-certified infrastructure providers: Google Sign-In (OAuth), Apple (APNs), Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), Twilio (SMS/Voice fail-safe), and Metered.ca (Encrypted TURN Relays). We never sell, monetize, or broker user data to advertising or marketing agencies.

8. Your Rights: Access, Export & Account Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data:

  • Instant Activity Deletion: Tap "Clear All Activity" in the app to purge all logs, voice notes, and photos immediately from the server.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, authentication records, household profiles, and all associated door placards directly within the app (Settings > Delete Account) or via our self-service Account Deletion Web Portal. Account deletion permanently scrubs all associated records within 60 seconds.
